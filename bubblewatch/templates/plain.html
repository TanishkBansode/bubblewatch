How to Make Sense of the AI Bubble Dashboard

You don't need a finance degree, a math background, or a statistics textbook to understand what's happening. Here is the entire system explained with simple analogies, interactive visuals, and zero jargon.

1 · What is an "AI Bubble", in plain words?

Imagine a popular bakery in town that invents a delicious new croissant. At first, customers love it and happily pay $4.

Then people start saying: "In 5 years, everyone on Earth will eat 10 croissants a day!" Speculators rush in, buying up ovens, flour, butter, and all the croissants they can find. The price of a croissant jumps to $20, then $80, then $250.

A bubble occurs when stock prices are driven by people rushing to buy because "prices are going up tomorrow", rather than by how much real cash the companies actually earn. When expectations get impossibly high, even tiny bad news can cause people to head for the exit at once.

The 3 Rules of Every Tech Cycle

  • The tech is real: The Internet was real in 1999; AI is real today. Bubbles don't form around junk; they form around genuine revolutions.
  • The timeline is exaggerated: Investors expect 15 years of future profits in 12 months.
  • The hardware builds first: Companies spend hundreds of billions on chips (Nvidia) and data centers before finding out if regular customers will pay enough for the software.

3 · The 6 Gauges Explained with Everyday Analogies

Where does the temperature come from? It listens to 6 different "heartbeats" in the financial system. Here is what each one actually listens for:

Gauge 1 · Weight 20%
Price Momentum
🏎️
"How fast is the car driving down the highway?"

Normal cruising speed is 65 mph. If AI stocks suddenly surge to 140 mph over a 60-day stretch, they might cover ground quickly, but any tiny obstacle on the road can cause a devastating crash. When momentum stretches way above normal, the gauge gets hot.

In the dashboard: Look at AI complex momentum 60d.
Gauge 2 · Weight 20%
Credit Complacency
🐤
"The canary in the coal mine: Do lenders care about risk?"

Stock investors can be emotional dreamers, but bond lenders are strict accountants. When bond lenders charge risky companies virtually the same low interest rate as rock-solid governments, nobody is respecting risk. That "complacency" is a classic late-stage bubble warning.

In the dashboard: Look at HY credit spread (OAS). Lower spread = hotter temperature.
Gauge 3 · Weight 20%
Closeness to the Peak
🧗
"Standing right at the edge of the roof."

If prices are sitting at their absolute 1-year high (0% drawdown), everyone who bought recently is in profit and feelings of invincibility run rampant. If prices are already down 20% from the peak, the euphoria has already drained away.

In the dashboard: Look at Drawdown from 250d high. 0% = standing right at the peak.
Gauge 4 · Weight 15%
Crowding into AI
🚢
"5,000 passengers all rushing to one side of the boat."

There are 500 great American companies (S&P 500). If money is only pouring into the 22 AI companies while banks, hospitals, grocery chains, and factories get completely ignored, the boat is tilting. When everyone owns the same trade, who is left to buy?

In the dashboard: Look at AI complex vs equal-weight S&P.
Gauge 5 · Weight 15%
Market Concentration
🏀
"A basketball team where 1 giant player scores every point."

When a tiny handful of mega-companies (Nvidia, Apple, Microsoft, Alphabet, Amazon) make up 35%+ of the entire stock market, the index isn't diversified anymore. If just one of those giants stumbles on earnings, the entire stock market falls with it.

In the dashboard: Look at Cap-vs-equal-weight spread (concentration).
Gauge 6 · Weight 10%
Risk Appetite (Crypto)
🎰
"Checking the crowd's gambling mood at the casino."

Bitcoin doesn't have data centers or earnings reports; it trades on pure animal spirits. When Bitcoin is zooming upward, it tells us that investors have excess cash and are in a fearless, speculative gambling mood.

4 · What Does the "Regime" Mean?

The dashboard translates the numbers into 5 clear states so you don't have to guess:

Healthy Growth
Expansion

Prices are rising in an orderly trend. No alarming signs of madness or credit stress. Enjoy the ride.

Danger Zone
Euphoria

Temperature is above 70. Everyone is talking about riches. Fragile conditions where risk/reward is worst.

The Warning Sign
Distribution

Smart institutions quietly sell into the enthusiasm while retail buyers are still eager. Short-term momentum turns down.

The Slide
Contraction

AI stocks drop 25%+ from their peaks. Reality sets in, valuations compress, and latecomers nurse losses.

The Opportunity
Capitulation / Recovery

Deep sell-off (>30% drop) where panic exhaust itself and prices start stabilizing. Historically the best entry zone.

6 · The 5-Day Prediction & Public Scorecard

Will AI stocks go up or down over the next 5 days?

Every morning, our machine learning model looks at current momentum, interest rates, and bond spreads, and calculates a percentage probability that AI stocks will be higher 5 trading days from now.

Unlike typical Wall Street gurus who hide their bad calls, every single forecast is written into a permanent public ledger. Once 5 days pass, the computer grades the prediction:

  • Accuracy: Did the stock basket actually go up when the model said it would?
  • Brier Score: A statistical accuracy penalty (golf rules: lower is better). Guessing a flat 50% coin flip gives a Brier score of 0.25. If the model can't beat 0.25, the scoreboard honestly admits it!

Why We Test Against A Coin Toss

Predicting short-term stock movements over 5 days is notoriously difficult because random news happens every day.

By constantly measuring ourselves against a permanent baseline ("what if we just guessed 50/50 every day?"), you can see with your own eyes whether the machine is actually providing an edge or if the market is purely random noise right now.

7 · How to Read the Dashboard in 30 Seconds

Whenever you visit the dashboard in the morning, just ask yourself these 4 questions:

  • 1
    Check the Temperature: Is it above 70 (froth danger) or below 25 (deep pessimism)? Between 40 and 65 is normal day-to-day market conditions.
  • 2
    Check the Regime Chip: Is it in expansion (steady uptrend) or has it shifted to distribution (smart money selling) or euphoria (parabolic melt-up)?
  • 3
    Check for Alerts: If credit spreads jump or big tech deceleration occurs, a red alert banner will pop up immediately.
  • 4
    Check the Gap in Chart 1: Look at the gap between the amber line (AI stocks) and the dashed grey line (regular companies). When that gap gets historically wide, crowding is at an extreme.

8 · Jargon Translator (Common Wall Street Terms)

Z-Score
A measure of how weird today's number is compared to the past year. A z-score of 0 is completely ordinary; +2 is extraordinarily high; -2 is extraordinarily low.
High-Yield Spread (OAS)
The extra interest percentage that risky companies have to pay compared to the US government. When this is tiny, lenders are being reckless and complacent.
Drawdown
How far prices have fallen from their peak. 0% means you are at the highest price in history. -20% means you are 20% below the peak.
Equal-Weight S&P (RSP)
An index where all 500 companies have an equal 0.2% say, showing what the "average" American business is doing without Apple or Nvidia dominating the score.
Hyperscalers
The mega-cloud providers: Microsoft, Alphabet (Google), Amazon AWS, and Meta. They purchase the vast majority of all AI infrastructure on Earth.
Rebased to 100
Starting all stock charts at the exact same starting line ($100) so you can easily see which one gained more ground over time.
