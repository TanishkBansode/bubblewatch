WHAT IS BUBBLEWATCH?

A small automated research desk for one question: how close is the AI trade to breaking — and when does it get cheap again?

The short version

A handful of mega-cap companies are pouring hundreds of billions of dollars a year into data centers, GPUs and power, on the bet that artificial intelligence revenue arrives fast enough to pay for it. That single trade now dominates equity indices, credit markets, utility buildouts and even memory prices (see our companion project, GlutClock). When one theme gets this big, its cooling curve matters to everyone.

BubbleWatch is a program that wakes up daily, reads public signals of froth or stress — momentum stretch in the AI complex, crowding vs the boring equal-weight market, concentration, high-yield credit complacency, proximity-to-highs, crypto risk appetite — compresses them into a single Bubble Temperature (0–100), classifies the regime, writes down a probability forecast for the next five days, and later scores itself against those forecasts publicly.

It is a thermometer, not an oracle. The point is calibration and early warning, not predictions to bet on.

  • Melt-ups end in stages: breadth deteriorates while index still rises (equal-weight lags), then credit stops flattering everything, then price breaks. The gauges track those stages in order.
  • Concentration cuts both ways. Top-heavy indexes mean passive money mechanically amplifies whatever the top-10 does — up and especially down.
  • Credit is the adult in the room. Equity investors can rationalize anything; high-yield bond investors demand payment. Spreads at record tights = no fear priced anywhere.
  • The fundamental anchor: hyperscaler capex and NVDA growth. If capex growth decelerates while temperature stays hot, that's the classic divergence that precedes breaks.

What BubbleWatch is not

  • Not investment advice. It times nothing perfectly and will be wrong often — publicly, via its scoreboard.
  • Not a crash alarm. Bubbles can stay hotter longer than any gauge can stay short. Temperature 80+ can persist for quarters.
  • Not sentiment from social media. Only prices, credit and reported fundamentals — revealed preference, not vibes.
