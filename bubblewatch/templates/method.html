Key Design Principles

  • Trading-Day Calendar Filter: Crypto (BTC) trades 24/7/365, while equities trade ~252 sessions a year. BubbleWatch filters market dates to active equity sessions so 20d, 60d, and 250d lookback windows represent genuine trading sessions rather than empty weekend pads.
  • Compounded Equal-Weight Returns: Baskets are built by compounding daily mean percentage returns rather than averaging raw share prices, ensuring constituent IPOs or listings (e.g. TLN) do not distort the index.
  • Strict Chronological Split: Supervised machine learning models train on the first 80% of historical sessions and validate on the final 20% out-of-sample test set. No data shuffling or forward leakage.
  • Cryptographic Ledger: Daily forecasts are hashed by feature content and committed to an append-only JSONL ledger before outcomes occur. Once the 5-day horizon elapses, actual basket moves join back for evaluation.

Honest Limitations

  • Single Generative AI Cycle In-Sample: The current generative AI buildout has only one major boom cycle in recent market data. Rolling statistics inevitably overlap this period. Treat temperature as an ordinal scale (rising/falling, cool/hot) rather than an absolute law.
  • Five-Day Direction Noise: Short-term 5-day equity market direction is largely dominated by micro news events. Out-of-sample Brier scores will hover near 0.24–0.26. The value lies in honest probability calibration, not claims of psychic forecasting.
  • Fundamentals Update Latency: CapEx and revenue figures update once every 90 days following quarterly 10-Q earnings releases.
