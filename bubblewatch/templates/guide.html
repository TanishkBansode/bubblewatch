READING THE NUMBERS

Every element of the dashboard, what it means, and how to act on it.

1 · Bubble temperature (the headline gauge)

A single number from 0 (ice) to 100 (blow-off). It is a weighted average of six z-scores — each gauge measures how unusual today's reading is versus the last ~250 trading days of itself:

gaugeweightmeasures
z_mom_stretch20%60-day return of the AI complex vs its own history — how stretched the move is
z_credit_tightness20%US high-yield spreads vs their history — abnormally TIGHT credit = nobody demands compensation for risk = classic late-cycle tell
z_dd_proximity20%how close prices sit to their 250-day high — near highs = fragile euphoria zone
z_rs_eqw15%AI complex outperformance vs equal-weight S&P — money crowding into one theme
z_concentration15%cap-weighted vs equal-weight spread — index concentration building (price-based proxy; see method page)
z_btc_risk10%BTC 30d momentum as a generic risk-appetite thermometer

Each z is clipped at ±2 and mapped so an average z of +2 → temperature 100, −2 → 0:

T = clip(50 + 25 × Σ(wᵢ·zᵢ) / Σ(wᵢ present), 0, 100)

If some gauges are missing (cold start), weights renormalize and "gauge coverage" shows how much backs the number.

bandrangeread it as
frost<25extreme pessimism; historically where reversals get interesting

euphoric≥75melt-up territory; risk/reward typically worst right here
Hot ≠ imminent crash. Bubbles stay hot far longer than skeptics expect. Temperature tells you where you are in the arc, not when it ends.

3 · Direction model card

P(AI complex up next 5d) — logistic regression on momentum, relative-strength, credit-change and volatility features, trained chronologically (80% train / 20% test). Unlike GlutClock's physical-price models, this trains immediately on two years of backfilled quotes.

  • Brier score: mean squared error of probability forecasts on yes/no outcomes. Guessing 50% forever scores ~0.25 on balanced events; lower is better.
  • majority baseline acc: accuracy of always guessing the more common outcome. If the model barely beats it, the dashboard admits it.

5 · Fundamentals anchor

Hyperscaler capex ($B/quarter) and NVDA revenue YoY are the real cash flows underneath the story. Rules of thumb:

  • NVDA YoY decelerating ≥15pts QoQ triggers an alert — growth deceleration at high valuations is the classic pre-break setup.
  • Capex still rising + temperature falling = market pricing trouble before companies report it.
  • The anchor updates only when new rows land in data/manual/hyperscaler_capex.csv — staleness is surfaced in days.

6 · Prediction scoreboard

Every run logs P(up) to an append-only ledger keyed by feature-hash. When horizons elapse, outcomes join back and both model and constant-0.50 baseline get scored by Brier and accuracy. Calibration beats confidence: a "70%" forecast should come true about 70% of the time.
