2 · Algorithmic, Probability & Model Limitations

Algorithmic Risk

2.1 Nature of the Bubble Temperature Score. The 0–100 Bubble Temperature is a synthetic heuristic index constructed from an unweighted and weighted mathematical combination of six rolling standardized scores (z-scores) computed over an empirical lookback window of approximately 250 trading days. The weights (20% momentum stretch, 20% credit tightness, 20% drawdown proximity, 15% equal-weight relative strength, 15% index concentration, 10% crypto speculative momentum) were chosen by heuristic design. The Temperature does not represent an absolute economic truth, does not measure underlying company cash flows, does not predict market tops or market bottoms, and cannot forecast the exact timing or magnitude of any market correction.

2.2 The 5-Day Direction Forecast Model. The direction model provides an estimate of P(AI complex up next 5d) using an L2-regularized logistic regression trained on chronological splits of past market sessions. You acknowledge that:

  • Noise & Random Walk: Short-term (5-day) equity price fluctuations exhibit very high entropy and noise, and often closely approximate a random walk. Model probabilities will frequently hover between 45% and 55%.
  • Baseline Performance: Predicting an uninformative constant probability of 0.50 yields an expected Brier score of 0.2500. A statistical model achieving a Brier score of 0.2450 does not represent an exploitable trading edge or an actionable market signal.
  • Omission of Unquantifiable Variables: The model cannot ingest real-time breaking geopolitical news, central bank speech nuances, corporate fraud revelations, military conflicts, or surprise regulatory bans.

2.3 Structural Breaks & Model Drift. Financial markets are non-stationary complex systems. Historical statistical relationships between variables (such as credit spreads and equity momentum) can break down permanently during new macroeconomic regimes, changes in interest rate policy, or structural shifts in the technology hardware supply chain. BubbleWatch models are subject to degradation, model drift, and misspecification.

2.4 Transparent Scoreboard Disclaimer. The public prediction scoreboard logged in data/ledger.jsonl is designed as an open-source research exercise in algorithmic accountability. It is not an audited track record of trading profits or an endorsement of any algorithmic trading system.

3 · Data Integrity, Third-Party Sources & Delays

Data Disclosures

3.1 Ingestion Sources. BubbleWatch obtains raw market and economic data from publicly accessible third-party APIs and sources:

  • Equity & Benchmark Quotes: Ingested daily from Yahoo! Finance and Stooq public endpoints.
  • High-Yield Credit & Volatility Data: Ingested from the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis Economic Data (FRED®) service (ICE BofA US High Yield Index Option-Adjusted Spread and Cboe VIX).
  • Quarterly CapEx & Revenue Growth: Transcribed from public corporate 10-Q and 10-K regulatory filings with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) for Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, Microsoft, and NVIDIA.

3.2 "AS IS" and "AS AVAILABLE" Basis. All data feeds, raw values, processed features, tables, charts, and downloadable artifacts are provided strictly on an "AS IS" and "AS AVAILABLE" basis. BubbleWatch makes no warranties, express or implied, regarding the accuracy, completeness, timeliness, reliability, suitability, or correctness of any data point.

3.3 Inherent Latency & Reporting Lags. Market data on this website is subject to delays:

  • Equity market closing prices are processed after market close and are not tick-by-tick real-time streaming data.
  • FRED High-Yield OAS readings are typically published with an inherent 1 to 3 business day reporting lag by the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis.
  • Hyperscaler CapEx numbers update only once every calendar quarter (approximately 90 days) following quarterly earnings releases and may be subject to subsequent corporate restatements or accounting changes.

3.4 Downtime & Pipeline Interruptions. BubbleWatch operates via automated GitHub Actions cron schedules. Automated runs may fail, be delayed, or be skipped due to upstream API rate limits, third-party network outages, server maintenance, or parsing errors. BubbleWatch provides no guarantee of continuous, uninterrupted, or timely publication.
